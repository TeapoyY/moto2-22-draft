# 22-0 Moto2 World Championship Draft

Draft 5 Moto2 legends across 6 eras and try to win every race of the 22-race 2026 Moto2 calendar.

## What is this

A single-HTML X-0 Sports Draft game where you build a fantasy Moto2 team by picking 5 legendary riders across the eras. Try to sweep all 22 races for the ultimate 22-0 record.

- 84 Moto2 legends (250cc era + Triumph Moto2)
- 6 eras spanning 1970s to 2026
- 22 races on the real 2026 Moto2 calendar (all road circuits)
- Modern 25-1 points system (since 2023)

## How to play

1. Click an era tab (70-89, 90-09, 10-14, 15-18, 19-22, 23-26)
2. Click 5 riders to draft
3. Click "Start Season" to begin
4. Click "Race" to simulate each race
5. Aim for 22-0 (perfect sweep)

## Era Synergy

Drafting multiple riders from the same era gives bonus OVR:
- 2 from same era: +1.5 OVR
- 3 from same era: +3.0 OVR
- 4 from same era: +4.5 OVR
- 5 from same era: +6.0 OVR

Encourages "dynasty team" strategy (e.g. all Pedro Acosta era riders).
